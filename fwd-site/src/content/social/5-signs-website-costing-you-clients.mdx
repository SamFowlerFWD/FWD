## LinkedIn

Most small business owners I speak to know, somewhere in the back of their mind, that their website isn't working the way it should.

They just haven't pinned down exactly what the problem is.

Here are the five signs I see most consistently — and if more than two apply to you, your website is probably costing you real business.

**1. It takes more than three seconds to load.**
Bounce rates increase sharply after three seconds. Google also uses page speed as a ranking signal, so a slow site means fewer visitors in the first place. You can check yours free at PageSpeed Insights. Mobile score is the one to look at.

**2. It doesn't work properly on mobile.**
Most business owners check their site on a desktop. The mobile version can be a completely different experience. Try using your own site as a new customer would on your phone. Find the contact details, read the pricing page, try to make an enquiry. If anything feels awkward, you'll know.

**3. The design doesn't build trust.**
People form opinions about credibility within seconds. An outdated design, inconsistent branding, and missing trust signals (testimonials, contact details, professional photography) tell a first-time visitor more about your business than your copy does.

**4. There's no clear call to action.**
What do you want someone to do after reading your homepage? If the answer is "ring me" or "email me", that action needs to be obvious and prominent. If it isn't, interested people close the tab and don't come back.

**5. You're not showing up in local search.**
If you're not on the first page when someone in your area searches for what you do, the website is invisible to them. Google Search Console shows exactly where you're ranking and what you're missing.
