## LinkedIn

I use AI tools every day. They have genuinely changed how I work.

But I also see what they can and cannot do for a small business website, and there is a significant gap between the marketing and the reality.

Here is the honest version.

**Where AI is genuinely useful for your website:**

AI writing tools have solved the blank page problem. Getting a first draft of your services page, FAQ, or blog post takes seconds. You still need to edit it — you absolutely should — but the starting point is there.

AI image generation is useful for concepts and placeholders. It is not a replacement for real photography, but it is better than the stock photos everyone else uses.

AI chatbots are improving. If you get the same five questions by email every week, a well-configured chatbot can handle them around the clock.

**Where AI website builders fall short:**

Performance. The platforms pitching AI-built websites are still building on template infrastructure with all the same bloat problems. A slow site actively damages your position in search results. Google measures this.

Accessibility. AI generates images. It does not generate meaningful alt text. That context has to come from a human who understands what the image is doing on the page.

Custom functionality. Earlier this year we built a booking system for a dog field that automatically generates Paxton gate access codes after payment. No AI builder was going to do that. No template platform handles it. Custom code was the only answer.

The question is not "can AI build me a website?" It can. The question is whether the result actually serves your business.

I have written the full breakdown on the blog, including where AI tools are worth using and where their limitations will surface quickly.
